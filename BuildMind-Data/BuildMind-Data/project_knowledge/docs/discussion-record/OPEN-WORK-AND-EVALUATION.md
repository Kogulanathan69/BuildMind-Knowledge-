Test plans must contain intentionally invalid designs as well as valid ones. Expected results need reasons and a pinned rule release. The proposed starter set was 20 briefs, 20 invalid plans, 50 evidence-linked questions and 10 reviewed BOQ examples. Those are targets, not existing audited counts.

Proposed nonfunctional targets in the BRD include unit round trips within 1 mm, visible edits within 500 ms on the reference device, 30 fps for the bounded scene, configurable solver time budget initially 60 s and most RAG answers within 15 s excluding declared outages/ingestion. Measure on stated hardware; do not present targets as benchmarks already achieved. Proposed pilot: at least five consenting users if available, baseline comparison before claiming time savings.
